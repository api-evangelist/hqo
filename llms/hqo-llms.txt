# HqO

> HqO is a Boston-based proptech company whose Real Estate Experience (REX) Platform helps commercial real estate owners, operators, and asset managers deliver great tenant and workplace experiences across their portfolios. The platform spans six suites — Intelligence, Experience, Tenant, Operations, Leasing, and Vendor — and manages 150M+ square feet of real estate. There is no self-serve public developer portal; programmatic API/SDK access is delivered through an Enterprise Development engagement, alongside SSO (SAML), webview, deep-link, and native integrations.
